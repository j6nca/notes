# Rating: ⭐⭐⭐

I would say the coolest thing about this cafe is the location. Located in re-purposed bank hall, this cafe balances a coziness with the grand atmosphere of the hallowed out space of the former RBC headquarters in Montreal. The few times I have been there it is immensely busy.
